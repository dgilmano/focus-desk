# Focus Desk 0.6.0

- Explore projects and tasks on the Desk map or switch to List. Drag tasks between projects and double-click a name to rename it.
- Use the microphone on Desk to dictate a new task or update an existing one. Review its project, individual next steps and optional suggestions before saving. Connect your OpenAI API key in the voice widget and set your drafting preferences in Settings.
- Click a step or drag it into Journal to record `Done: <step>`. Drag a Journal entry back to Next step, or use its return button, to put it back on your list.

Personal update for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Your existing workspace stays in place. Microphone access is requested when you start recording. Transcribe sends the recording to OpenAI, and Create draft sends the note and your drafting instructions. Updating a task also sends its title, description and next steps.
