# Focus Desk 0.7.2

- Task map remembers which branches you expanded when you switch pages or reopen Focus Desk. Subtasks and next steps return to their saved positions.
- Right-click a task or subtask to start, pause, resume or stop its timer. Day tracker records the time under the task and activity you choose.
- Each task fills with green as you spend time on it today. The fill reaches halfway at 30 minutes and the full width at one hour. The task menu shows the recorded time even after the bar fills.

Personal update for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.
