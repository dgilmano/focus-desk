# Focus Desk 0.7.0

- Right-click a task on the Desk map and choose **Add subtask…**. Double-click the task to expand or collapse its subtasks and next steps.
- Drag an expanded next step anywhere on the map. Its line follows the card, and Focus Desk remembers its position relative to its task when you reopen the branch or restart the app.
- To rename a project, task, subtask or next step, click its name twice with a short pause. Edit it in place, then press Return to save or Escape to cancel.
- Choose a parent when creating a task on Desk, or choose **Subtask** in Voice note before dictating. **Current task** shows the project and parent task path. Task pickers in Voice note and Day tracker also show the hierarchy.

Personal update for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.
