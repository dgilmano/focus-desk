# Focus Desk 0.7.3

- Double-click the center dot of a project cloud in Task map to hide its tasks, subtasks and next steps. Double-click again to show them in their saved positions.
- Right-click a cloud to rename it, choose from ten colors, or add, change or remove its icon. Focus Desk remembers each cloud’s appearance and whether it is collapsed when you switch pages or reopen the app.
- The Untagged cloud now has a Delete action. Focus Desk shows how many unassigned tasks will be removed, and Undo restores those tasks and their journal entries.

Personal update for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.
